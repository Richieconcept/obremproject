Our Core Services

At OBREM TECH LIMITED, we offer a range of specialized services tailored to meet our clients' unique needs in the field of vertical and horizontal transportation systems.

Sales

We sell different brands of vertical/horizontal transportation equipment and UPS equipment, customized to meet the specific brand preferences of our customers.

Installation

Our team of certified and experienced engineers is capable of installing any brand of vertical/horizontal transportation equipment and UPS equipment, ensuring top-notch service.

Consultancy Service

We provide expert advice on the design, testing, and maintenance inspection of elevators, escalators, moving walkways, and various conveyances for safe and efficient people movement.

After Sales Service

At OBREM TECH LIMITED, we pride ourselves on offering comprehensive after-sales services, ensuring that your transportation systems continue to operate at peak performance.

Repairs and Overhauling

We provide rapid response to breakdowns, overhauling, and ensure the availability of spare parts to get your systems up and running quickly.

Maintenance

Our maintenance services come in two types:
Comprehensive Maintenance: Covers the cost of spare parts used during service and breakdown calls.
Preventive Maintenance: Regular service without the cost of spare parts.

Stand-By Operator/In-House Technician

Upon customer request, we provide a dedicated lift operator or in-house technician to ensure smooth operations of the lift systems.

Modernization/Refurbishment

We upgrade critical parts of your elevator to improve performance, safety, and aesthetics while integrating new technology for better functionality.

Certified and Award Winning Elevator Service Provider

We are committed to providing dependable services that minimize downtime and ensure uninterrupted operation of your transportation systems. Our clients can trust us to deliver on time, every time..
